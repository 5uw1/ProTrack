# WorkTracker – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 September 2026_

WorkTracker is a time-tracking app by suw1labs. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

## What the app stores

Projects, tasks, clock-in periods, activities, absences and your work schedule are stored in a
database inside the app's private storage on your device. The app has no user accounts and no
server of its own.

## What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you ask for it:

* **Export** – CSV reports and JSON backups are created only when you tap Save or Share, and go
  where you send them (a file location, an e-mail, a chat).
* **Automatic backup** – if you choose a backup folder (for example Google Drive or iCloud
  Drive), the app writes a JSON copy of your data into that folder after each change. The folder
  belongs to your account with that provider; its privacy terms apply to files stored there.
* **Notifications** – deadline reminders and the "still clocked in" reminder are scheduled
  locally on the device. No push service is used.

## What the app does not do

* No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting.
* No network requests. The app works fully offline.
* No access to contacts, location, camera or microphone.

## Permissions

* **Notifications** (Android 13+, iOS) – to show reminders. Optional.
* **Folder access** – only for the backup folder you pick, via the system folder picker.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes its database. Backup files you created in a folder of your choice
stay there until you delete them.
