# Privacy Policy – WorkTracker

_Last updated: 16 September 2026_

WorkTracker is a personal working-time tracker. **It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.**

- All data you enter (clock-in times, activities, projects, absences, settings) is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private database.
- The app has **no server**, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
- Exports (CSV files) are created only when you ask for it and go only where you send them (your files, e-mail, Teams, …).
- Notifications are local reminders generated on your device.
- No account is required.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.
