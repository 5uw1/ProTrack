WorkTracker is a time tracker for engineers and consultants who have to book their hours on projects – and want to spend as little time doing it as possible.

CLOCK IN, CLOCK OUT
• One tap in the morning, one at lunch, one when you leave. Only clocked-in time counts.
• Tag a pause as lunch or break; paid short breaks are credited automatically.
• Forgot to clock out? The app reminds you and fixes it with one tap.

TRACK PROJECTS AND TASKS
• Pick a project and task while you work; switching starts the next activity.
• Edit times afterwards – move the neighbouring activity along or leave a gap and assign it later. No minute is lost.
• Clocked-in time without an activity is shown on the day's timeline and can be assigned in one tap.

REPORTS THAT MATCH YOUR TIMESHEET
• Day, week and month views with hours per project number, rounded to 0.25 h.
• Target hours, overtime today and the running overtime balance.
• Absences: sick, holiday, public holiday, compensation, education.
• Export CSV for a spreadsheet or your ERP system.

WORKING-TIME RULES
• Configurable schedule per weekday (part-time friendly), legal maximum per week.
• Warnings for missing breaks, long days and weeks over the limit (Swiss rules by default).

WIDGETS
• Home-screen widget with today's clocked-in time, the running activity, Clock in / Clock out and one-tap task switching.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything is stored on your device. No account, no server, no tracking.
• Optional automatic backup into a folder you choose – Google Drive, iCloud Drive or a local folder – and one-file restore on any device.
• Also available for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Languages: English, German, French.
